What Cosmic Mirror Does

Daily Horoscope

Cosmic Mirror uses your date of birth to calculate your zodiac sign and personalize a simple daily reading.

  • Get a daily horoscope based on your zodiac sign
  • Use the app without creating an account
  • Keep your date of birth and app activity stored locally on your device
  • Use a widget to show a small local horoscope summary

Common Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Cosmic Mirror does not require an account.

Is my horoscope saved online?

No. Your date of birth and app activity are stored locally on your device.

Why does my horoscope change each day?

Cosmic Mirror uses daily astrology data to create a simple daily reading.

How do I reset the app?

Delete and reinstall the app to clear local data.

Troubleshooting

If Something Is Not Working

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check that the latest version is installed
  • Make sure your device has an internet connection for daily astrology data
  • Delete and reinstall the app if you want to clear local data

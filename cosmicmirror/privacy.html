Privacy Policy for Cosmic Mirror

Last updated: April 28, 2026. Cosmic Mirror is designed to be simple and private.

Information We Collect

Cosmic Mirror asks for your date of birth so it can calculate your zodiac sign and personalize your daily horoscope.

This information is stored locally on your device. We do not create accounts, collect names, collect email addresses, or store your date of birth on our servers.

Data and Storage

Network Requests

Cosmic Mirror uses a third-party astrology API to retrieve daily astrology data. The app may send the selected date and general astrology request data to this API. Your name, email address, or account information is not sent because the app does not collect those details.

Local Storage

Cosmic Mirror stores app preferences locally on your device, including your date of birth, daily view streak, and cached horoscope data.

Widgets

If you use the Cosmic Mirror widget, a small summary of your horoscope may be stored locally in an app group so the widget can display it.

Use and Sharing

Analytics and Advertising

Cosmic Mirror does not use advertising tracking.

Cosmic Mirror does not currently use third-party analytics.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Legal

Children's Privacy

Cosmic Mirror is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

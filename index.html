Features

What Shawty Does

Shawty helps you:

  • Log doses and injections
  • Set protocols and schedules
  • Calculate reconstitution and syringe units
  • Track symptoms and side effects
  • Monitor weight and progress over time
  • Manage inventory, vial usage, and remaining supply
  • View timelines, charts, and summaries in a clean visual interface

FAQ

Is my data stored online?

Shawty is designed as a local-first app. Your logs and tracking data stay on your device unless you choose to export or share something yourself.

Does Shawty connect to Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission, Shawty can read relevant data such as weight from Apple Health to enrich progress tracking.

Does Shawty give medical advice?

No. Shawty is a tracking and organization tool. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace a licensed medical professional.

How do I report a bug?

Email kanishk.chores@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Privacy & Legal

Privacy

Shawty is built to feel private, calm, and lightweight. Any Apple Health access is permission-based and used only to support the app's tracking features.

Medical Disclaimer

Shawty is intended for tracking and personal organization only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health-related decisions.

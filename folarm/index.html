What Folarm Does

Mission-Based Alarms

Folarm turns alarms into wake-up missions. Instead of simply tapping stop, you complete a small challenge like taking an awake selfie, capturing the sky, making the bed, or finishing a quick physical task.

  • Set iPhone alarms with a wake-up mission attached
  • Choose missions designed to get your mind and body moving
  • Complete the mission to finish the wake-up flow
  • Use the app without sign-up, login, or cloud account setup

Common Help Topics

1. My alarm did not ring

Please make sure your iPhone is running the latest supported iOS version, notifications are allowed for Folarm, and any required alarm permissions are enabled. Also make sure your volume is on and the alarm was saved successfully.

2. Camera missions are not working

Folarm needs camera access for missions like awake selfies or photo-based wake checks. You can enable camera access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Folarm.

3. I denied a permission by mistake

You can update permissions anytime in the iPhone Settings app. Open Settings, find Folarm, and enable the permissions you want to use.

4. My mission is too difficult in the morning

You can edit or replace the mission when creating or editing an alarm.

5. Does Folarm need an account?

No. Folarm is designed to work without sign-up, login, or cloud account setup.

6. Does Folarm store my data online?

Folarm is built to be local-first. Your app data is stored on your device unless future features explicitly say otherwise.

Troubleshooting

If Folarm Is Not Behaving As Expected

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check that the latest version is installed
  • Review permissions in iPhone Settings
  • Delete and recreate the alarm
  • Restart your iPhone if needed

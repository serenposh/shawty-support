What Folarm Does

Wake Up Selfie Alarms

Folarm lets you schedule alarms on iPhone and complete a Wake Up Selfie flow when an alarm rings. The selfie check is designed to help confirm that you are awake before finishing the alarm flow.

  • Schedule alarms on iPhone
  • Complete a Wake Up Selfie when the alarm rings
  • Use camera permission for selfie capture
  • Use the app without an account, login, subscription, or in-app purchases

Common Help Topics

1. My alarm did not ring

Please make sure your iPhone is running a supported iOS version, Folarm has alarm authorization, and the alarm was saved successfully. Also check your device volume, Focus settings, and whether the alarm time and repeat schedule are correct.

2. Camera permission is not working

Folarm needs camera access for the Wake Up Selfie flow. You can enable camera access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Folarm, or in Settings > Apps > Folarm.

3. Alarm permissions are not enabled

Folarm uses iOS alarm authorization, including AlarmKit where available, to schedule alarms. If alarm authorization was denied or disabled, open iPhone Settings, find Folarm, and enable the permissions required for alarms.

4. Wake Up Selfie is not working

Make sure camera access is enabled, your face and eyes are clearly visible, and the room has enough light. If OpenAI verification is configured for selfie checks, make sure your internet connection is working.

5. Does Folarm need an account?

No. Folarm does not require account creation, login, subscription, or in-app purchases.

6. How does Folarm handle privacy and data?

Folarm stores core alarm data and app preferences on your device. If OpenAI verification is configured for Wake Up Selfie, a captured selfie may be sent to OpenAI only to help determine whether you appear awake. Folarm does not use this to identify you or create a biometric profile.

Troubleshooting

If Folarm Is Not Behaving As Expected

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check that the latest version is installed
  • Review alarm and camera permissions in iPhone Settings
  • Check your internet connection if Wake Up Selfie verification uses OpenAI
  • Delete and recreate the alarm
  • Restart your iPhone if needed

Bug Reports

If something is not working as expected, email support with:

  • Your iPhone model
  • iOS version
  • Folarm version
  • The alarm time and what happened
  • A short description of the issue
  • Screenshots if helpful

Privacy Policy for Folarm

Effective date: April 27, 2026. Folarm respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Folarm handles information when you use the app.

Overview

Folarm is designed to be a private, local-first alarm app. It does not require an account to use the core product. We aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

Local App Data

Folarm may store the following information locally on your device in order to function:

  • Alarm settings
  • Repeat schedules
  • Selected wake-up missions
  • Mission history
  • Streaks and basic in-app progress
  • App preferences and settings

Camera Access

Folarm may request access to your device camera for wake-up missions such as awake selfies or other photo-based tasks. Camera access is used only for app functionality.

If the app stores any captured images, those images are stored locally on your device unless stated otherwise inside the app. Folarm does not require a cloud account for these features.

Account, Sharing, and Storage

No Account Required

Folarm does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its core features.

Data Sharing

Folarm does not sell your personal information.

Folarm does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or remote servers to transmit your personal data as part of normal core use.

If future versions add optional third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Data Storage

Folarm is designed to store core app data locally on your device. Because of this, deleting the app may also delete locally stored app data unless your device backup settings preserve it.

Permissions and Choices

Notifications and Permissions

Folarm may request permissions needed to provide its features, such as notifications and camera access.

You can manage permissions at any time in your iPhone Settings.

Your Choices

You can disable permissions in iPhone Settings, delete alarms and app data inside the app where supported, or delete the app from your device.

Legal

Children's Privacy

Folarm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.

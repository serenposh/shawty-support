Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Shawty, a local-first iPhone app for tracking peptides and GLP-1 workflows.

Overview

Shawty is a local-first iPhone app designed to help users track peptides and GLP-1 workflows, including doses, schedules, symptoms, progress, and inventory.

Data & Storage

Local Data Storage

Shawty stores user-entered data locally on the device.

Apple Health

If Apple Health access is enabled by the user, Shawty may read relevant health data such as weight to support progress tracking.

Data Use

No Data Sales

Shawty does not sell personal data.

No Third-Party Advertising

Shawty does not use user data for third-party advertising.

AI-Powered Features

If AI-powered features are used, selected user inputs such as photos or text entered for those features may be sent securely to a model provider only to perform the requested analysis or summary.

Legal

Medical Disclaimer

Shawty is intended for tracking and organization only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
